# Contact Us

Have a question, feature request, or bug report? We'd love to hear from you.

## Email

## Support

### For App Issues

1. Check the [User Manual](/docs/) (common issues covered)
2. Email **rftransmission.net@gmail.com** with:
   - What you were trying to do
   - What happened instead
   - Browser and OS
   - Screenshot (if applicable)


### For RF/Technical Questions

1. Check the [Technical Reference](/docs/)
2. Email rftransmission.net@gmail.com with your question
